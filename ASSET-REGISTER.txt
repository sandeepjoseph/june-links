COURSE DIRECTORY
- public/assets/courses.json: Wikidata golf-course records, CC0 1.0, retrieved 2026-11-03.
  It holds 1,306 references across 61 countries and territories, with US states added from
  Wikidata's administrative hierarchy. It's a planning reference, not live inventory.
  https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing
- public/assets/venue-media.json: venue photo records from Wikidata (P18/P625) and Wikimedia
  Commons. Only CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC0 and public-domain files are included, and each record
  carries its author and license. Images load on demand through the site's own photo route;
  browsers never supply a remote URL. Rebuild: scripts/source-venue-media.py.
- public/assets/standrews-800.webp: Optograph, CC BY-SA 3.0. The derivative keeps that license.
