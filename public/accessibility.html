Using the site

June Links provides keyboard navigation, visible focus states, descriptive image text and labeled inquiry fields. The form announces errors and moves focus to each new step.

Motion

The films and course gallery have pause controls. The inquiry paper animation runs once and follows your device’s reduced motion setting. The site follows the W3C reduced motion technique for that effect.

Feedback

If you have difficulty using the site, email info@junelinks.com with the page and task you were trying to complete.
