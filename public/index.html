The best base works for the golfers and the people joining them. Think about what everyone would enjoy while a round is under way.

Plan time off the course

Before you book

Is June Links a golf course or tour operator?

June Links is an independent travel broker. Courses, hotels, transport companies and experience providers operate their own services. Your proposal will explain which supplier provides each service and who takes payment.

Can non-golfers come along?

Absolutely. Tell us how many people will play and how many won’t. The Signature tier can include requests for sightseeing, dining and other activities, so everyone has a reason to look forward to the trip.

Are the featured courses and tee times guaranteed?

No. The destinations and courses shown are ideas to discuss. Tee times, access, course conditions and transport remain subject to supplier confirmation. We’ll discuss alternatives before you accept a proposal.

How much will my trip cost?

Dates, courses, group size and the level of care all affect the price. Choose the spending comfort that suits your group, from $ to $$$$. Your written proposal will show costs, any broker fee or supplier commission arrangements, deposits and exclusions before you commit.

Can I play every day, or only a few days?

Either works as a starting point. Let us know your nights away, intended golf days and any 36-hole days. We’ll consider travel time, arrival and departure days, course access and the needs of the whole group.

What about changes, weather and cancellation?

Each supplier sets its own cancellation, refund, weather and no-show rules. We’ll provide the applicable terms before confirmation. Travel insurance is worth considering separately; this website does not sell an insurance policy.
