Course listings show available photo credits alongside each image.

The golf film

Three recorded aerial views are edited into one background film: Logan Voss, coastal golf at Rancho Palos Verdes; David Pickup, parkland golf and a lake; and Sergio Benitez, Hole 17. All are used under the Pexels License. The edit uses gentle dissolves, resizing and compression. The recorded landscapes and camera motion are retained.

The film provides golf-travel inspiration. It does not identify a featured itinerary or imply a June Links supplier partnership. View the film.

The globe

Earth image: NASA Goddard Space Flight Center, Reto Stöckli and Robert Simmon; Land, ocean and ice, CC BY-SA 3.0. The image is displayed on a shaded, rotating globe. The Earth image and adapted renderings of it remain under that license.

The course collection

The Ireland, Scotland, USA and Mexico photographs were supplied by June Links. They depict Royal County Down, Old Petty at Cabot Highlands, Pebble Beach and Cabo Del Sol. The England photograph shows Royal Lytham & St Annes. Wojciech Migda, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0. The resized WebP and cropped display retain that license.
