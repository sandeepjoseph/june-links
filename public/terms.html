Terms & Booking Information

Updated November 5, 2026

Our role

June Links is an independent golf travel broker. We help plan golf trips and coordinate options with the independent providers that deliver the services.

Inquiries and booking

This website accepts planning inquiries. Bookings and payments are arranged separately after you review and accept the written proposal and the relevant suppliers confirm availability.

What the proposal covers

The Round covers golf planning. The Journey adds ground transport planning. The Signature adds accommodation and selected experiences. Before you commit, the proposal identifies the included services, suppliers, contracting party and payment recipient. It sets out prices, currency, fees, taxes, deposits and any material commercial relationships relevant to the booking.

Changes and cancellations

Review the supplier terms for access, deposits, changes, cancellations, weather interruptions and refunds before confirming. Those terms apply to the supplier’s services, subject to applicable consumer rights. Check the itinerary and entry requirements for every traveler before departure.

Photography and other materials

The photo and film credits identify image sources and licensing information. For a question about these terms, email info@junelinks.com.
