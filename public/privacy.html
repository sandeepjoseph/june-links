Privacy Notice

Updated November 5, 2026

What we collect

June Links uses the contact and trip details you submit to respond to your inquiry and prepare planning options. We ask for your name, email, preferred service tier and confirmation that you are at least 18. Phone, departure city, contact timing and other trip preferences are optional. Leave payment details, identification documents and medical information out of the form.

How your information is used

The site saves your inquiry and provides a reference and downloadable trip brief. Relevant trip details may be shared with independent providers to check availability or prepare a proposal. Hosting providers process connection and request information. When email delivery is configured, the email provider processes the inquiry brief for delivery to June Links.

Preview storage

Use test details on this review site. Inquiries and associated delivery records are stored in the preview database until the owner removes them. The service keeps short-lived rate-limit records to reduce abuse. Inquiry details are not saved in browser storage. Sending an inquiry does not subscribe you to marketing.

Location and privacy requests

If you use the optional device location control and grant permission, approximate coordinates are used for the route preview in your browser. Your selected departure city, if provided, is included in the inquiry.

To ask about access, correction or deletion of your information, email info@junelinks.com. Include your inquiry reference if available. We may verify your identity before acting on the request.
